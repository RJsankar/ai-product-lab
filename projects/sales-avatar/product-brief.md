# Product Brief

## Problem

Sales reps need realistic, low-risk practice before customer conversations.

## User

Sales representatives and field representatives preparing for customer meetings.

## Goal

Simulate a credible customer conversation and deliver specific feedback a rep can apply in the next attempt.

## MVP

- Select a persona and scenario
- Converse with an AI that stays in character
- Encounter relevant objections
- End the simulation on demand
- Receive strengths, misses, a score, and suggested improvements

## Not in the MVP

- Voice or animated avatars
- Multiple agents
- CRM integrations
- Full retrieval-augmented generation

## Success metric

A user can complete a realistic simulation and rates the feedback as useful for improving the next conversation.
