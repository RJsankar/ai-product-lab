# Evaluation Plan

Evaluation is part of the MVP, not a later quality layer.

## Simulation quality

- Persona consistency
- Scenario relevance
- Realism of objections
- Conversational coherence

## Coaching quality

- Evidence from the transcript
- Specificity of feedback
- Actionability of recommendations
- Consistency of scoring

## Initial rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5:

1. Discovery and questioning
2. Listening and relevance
3. Value articulation
4. Objection handling
5. Clear next step

## Early validation

Run a small set of scripted strong, average, and weak conversations. Compare model scores with human judgment, record failure cases, and refine prompts before expanding scope.
